# Technical Scope Document — Heat Exchanger Replacement (fictional)

## 1. Background

The Harborview Generating Station requires replacement of two shell-and-tube
heat exchangers on the closed cooling water system. The existing units are at
end of life and spare parts are no longer available.

## 2. Scope of work

The design engineering services required are:

- A **Design Plan** covering the replacement approach, deliverables, and
  schedule for the detailed design stage.
- A **Walkdown Report** documenting the site walkdown of the existing heat
  exchangers, piping interfaces, and access constraints.
- A **Technical Specification** for procurement of the replacement heat
  exchangers, including performance requirements and materials.
- A **Design Manual** update capturing the as-built configuration after
  installation.
- A **Vendor Documentation Review** of the heat exchanger vendor's drawings
  and datasheets.
- **Closeout** of the design package, including final review and records
  archiving.

## 3. Engineering stages and disciplines

- Stage: Detailed design.
- Disciplines: Mechanical and electrical / instrumentation & control.

## 4. Schedule

The design package is required within 12 weeks of award. The walkdown must be
completed within the first 3 weeks to confirm interfaces before the
specification is issued.
